How this drill works

Every problem is one of the 14 templates in the course handout Numerical Problems and Solutions (Midterm, 2026). The wording of the problem and of the solution steps is fixed. Only the numbers change, and they are drawn from the whole-number ranges below so the problems can be worked by hand. Solutions are computed by formula, not by an AI model. Pick a topic and, if you want, a specific problem to drill; Try a similar problem keeps the same structure and redraws the numbers. Keyboard: S shows the solution, R tries a similar problem, N loads the next problem.
